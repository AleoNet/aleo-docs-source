# Validators

Validators are the nodes that produce blocks, verify zero-knowledge proofs, and participate in [AleoBFT consensus](/learn/network/consensus). They are the backbone of the Aleo network.

## Role

Validators are responsible for:

- **Verifying ZK proofs** — Checking that transitions were executed correctly without seeing the underlying data
- **Executing async functions** — Running onchain logic (mapping updates) after proof verification
- **Producing blocks** — Participating in AleoBFT consensus to commit transactions
- **Maintaining state** — Storing the ledger, mappings, and encrypted records

## Committee

The active set of validators is called the **committee**, capped at **200 validators**. Membership is determined by total stake — the top 200 by stake are included. Voting power within the committee is proportional to stake.

A validator must meet the following requirements to join the committee:

| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| Total stake (self-bond + delegations) | 10,000,000 Aleo Credits |
| Self-bond minimum | 100 Aleo Credits |

If a validator's total stake falls below 10M credits or their self-bond drops below 100 credits, they are removed from the committee.

## Rewards

Validators receive **1/3 of coinbase rewards** as block rewards, distributed proportionally to their stake within the committee. Validators also set a **commission rate** (0–100%) which determines what percentage of delegator rewards they retain.

## Configuration

When bonding as a validator, operators set:

- **Commission rate** — Percentage of delegator rewards kept by the validator
- **Withdrawal address** — The address where unbonded stake is returned
- **Open/closed status** — Whether new delegations are accepted

## Staking

Staking on Aleo is handled natively through the `credits.aleo` program. Users can delegate credits to validators to participate in securing the network and earn a share of block rewards.

### Key Parameters

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Delegation minimum | 10,000 credits |
| Minimum bonded balance | 10,000 credits |
| Unbonding period | 360 blocks |
| Max delegators (network-wide) | 100,000 |

### Delegating

Any user can delegate credits to a validator using the `bond_public` function. Delegated stake counts toward the validator's total stake for committee membership and voting power.

- Delegators automatically earn a share of block rewards proportional to their stake, minus the validator's commission
- A validator must be **open** to new delegations for `bond_public` to succeed
- The minimum delegation is 10,000 credits

### Unbonding

Stakers can unbond some or all of their stake using `unbond_public`. This starts a **360-block cooldown** (~2.5 hours at 25-second blocks). After the cooldown, anyone can call `claim_unbond_public` to transfer the unbonded credits to the staker's withdrawal address.

- Partial unbonding is allowed, but if the remaining bonded balance falls below 10,000 credits, a full unbond is triggered
- If a validator's total stake drops below 10M credits, they are removed from the committee

### Withdrawal Address

Each staker has a **withdrawal address** — the destination for unbonded credits. This is set when bonding and can be different from the staking address, allowing operators to separate operational and custodial concerns.
