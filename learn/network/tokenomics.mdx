# Tokenomics

## Initial Supply

The Aleo network launched with a genesis supply of **1.5 billion ALEO credits**, minted at the genesis block on September 4, 2024.

## Emission Sources

New credits enter circulation through two mechanisms:

- **Block rewards** — Paid to validators and their delegators for producing blocks. Targets approximately **5% annual inflation** on the current total supply, yielding ~75M credits/year at genesis.
- **Coinbase rewards** — Paid to provers (2/3) and validators (1/3) based on puzzle solutions submitted in each block.

Transaction fees are also collected by validators but are not new issuance — they are transfers from existing supply.

## Reward Split

Each block's coinbase reward is divided:

| Recipient | Share | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Provers** | 2/3 | Distributed proportionally to each prover's proof target contribution |
| **Validators** | 1/3 | Added to the block reward and distributed to the validator and their delegators by stake |


## Coinbase Reward Schedule

Coinbase rewards decrease over a 10-year schedule. The maximum possible coinbase reward per block (**R_anchor**) declines linearly, reaching a fixed minimum (**R_Y9**) in year 9 that remains constant thereafter until the hard cap.

The actual coinbase reward for a given block depends on how much proving activity occurred:

```
R_coinbase = R_anchor × min(P, C_remaining) / C
```

Where **P** is the combined proof target from all solutions, **C** is the coinbase target, and **C_remaining** is how much of the epoch's target has not yet been filled. In practice, proving activity is well below the theoretical maximum, so actual emissions are lower than the table above.

### Supply Cap (ARC-0047)

Per [ARC-0047](https://github.com/ProvableHQ/ARCs/discussions/101), the total supply of Aleo credits is hard-capped at **5 billion**. At block height **263,527,685** (estimated ~Q4 2049), both block rewards and coinbase rewards drop to zero. No new credits are minted after this point.

The cutoff height is calculated assuming worst-case proving activity at every block. Since actual emissions are consistently below the theoretical maximum, the real supply at cutoff will be well under 5B. This design provides long-term monetary predictability without requiring runtime supply tracking.
