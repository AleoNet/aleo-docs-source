# Faucets

## Aleo Credits

Get 20 Aleo Credits on Testnet from the official faucet at [faucet.aleo.org](https://faucet.aleo.org/).

## USDCx

USDCx on Testnet is bridged from Sepolia. You will need an EVM-compatible wallet (e.g., MetaMask) with:

1. **Sepolia ETH** — for gas fees. Available from any Sepolia faucet.
2. **Sepolia USDC** — available from the [Circle Faucet](https://faucet.circle.com/).

Once you have both, bridge your USDC to Testnet at [usdcx.aleo.dev](https://usdcx.aleo.dev/).

## USAD

USAD on Testnet is bridged from Sepolia. You will need an EVM-compatible wallet with:

1. **Sepolia ETH** — for gas fees. Available from any Sepolia faucet.
2. **Sepolia USDG** — available from the [Paxos Faucet](https://faucet.paxos.com/).

Once you have both, bridge your USDG to Testnet at [usad.aleo.dev](https://usad.aleo.dev/).
