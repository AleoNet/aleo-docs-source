Built by Provable in partnership with the Aleo Network Foundation, Shield is the recommended wallet for interacting with the Aleo ecosystem.

- **Record Scanning** — Automatically discovers and decrypts your private records onchain
- **Delegated Proving** — Proof generation is handled in a privacy-preserving way server-side, so private transactions are fast
- **Feemaster** — Private transactions and shielding of assets are fee-free
- **Private Stablecoins** — Deep integrations with [USDCx](https://usdcx.aleo.org/) and [USAD](https://usad.aleo.org/) for private stablecoin transfers
- **Crosschain Swaps** — Swap assets across chains via [NEAR Intents](https://near-intents.org/) and [Houdini Swap](https://app.houdiniswap.com/)

## Embedded Wallets

Embedded wallets are non-custodial wallets created and managed within your application. Users authenticate with familiar methods (email, social login, passkeys) and a wallet is provisioned automatically — no browser extension or external app required.
